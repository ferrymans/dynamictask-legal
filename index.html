Privacy Policy — Dynamic Todo (灵动代办)

Last updated: 2026-05-13

简体中文

我们收集什么数据

零数据。Dynamic Todo 不收集任何用户数据到我们的服务器,也不传送给任何第三方。

数据如何存储

  • 本地存储:所有代办、目标、计划、维度、灵感数据保存在你 iOS 设备的 SwiftData 数据库中。
  • iCloud 同步:若你登录 iCloud 并启用 iCloud Drive,数据通过 Apple CloudKit 私有数据库在你自己的设备间同步。只有你和 Apple 可访问该数据,Dynamic Todo 开发者和任何第三方都无法访问。

系统权限

  • 提醒事项(可选):在你主动点击同步按钮且授权后,我们才会将代办写入 iOS「提醒事项」App。可在 设置 → 隐私 → 提醒事项 内随时撤销。
  • 通知:番茄钟完成时发送本地通知,完全在你设备内运行。
  • iCloud(自动):首次启动自动开启,用于跨设备同步。

第三方服务

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit:数据在你的设备间同步。详见 Apple 隐私政策。
  • Apple StoreKit:订阅与购买处理。我们永远看不到你的支付信息。
  • Apple EventKit(可选):仅在你授权且主动同步代办时调用,仅写入 iOS 提醒事项 App,不与外部服务器通信。

家庭共享

若你或家庭组织者为订阅 / 永久买断启用 Family Sharing,家庭成员可通过共享 entitlement 使用 Pro 功能。支付信息仅家庭组织者可见。

儿童

Dynamic Todo 分级为 4+,适合所有年龄。我们不会知晓地收集任何人的数据,无论年龄。

政策变更

本隐私政策若有重大变更,我们将通过 App 内通知或新版本更新说明告知。

English

What data we collect

Zero data. Dynamic Todo collects no user data to our servers, and transmits no data to any third party.

How data is stored

  • On-device: All todos, goals, plans, dimensions, and inspirations are stored locally in your iOS device's SwiftData database.
  • iCloud Sync: If you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled, your data syncs across your own devices via Apple CloudKit private database. Only you and Apple can access this data — the Dynamic Todo developer and any third party cannot.

System permissions

  • Reminders (optional): We only write todos to iOS Reminders after you explicitly tap the sync button and grant permission. Revoke anytime via Settings → Privacy → Reminders.
  • Notifications: Local notifications when Pomodoro sessions complete. Runs entirely on-device.
  • iCloud (automatic): Enabled on first launch for cross-device sync.

Third-party services

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit: Cross-device data sync. See Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Apple StoreKit: Subscription and purchase processing. We never see your payment information.
  • Apple EventKit (optional): Invoked only when you authorize and explicitly sync a todo. Writes only to iOS Reminders, no external server communication.

Family Sharing

If you or a family organizer enables Family Sharing for the subscription / lifetime purchase, family members can use Pro features via shared entitlement. Payment information is visible only to the family organizer.

Children

Dynamic Todo is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Policy changes

Material changes to this Privacy Policy will be announced via in-app notification or new version release notes.
